# DeSign Mage 0.4.3

- Auto-save: if the program closes before you save, the next start offers to bring your design back
- Effects follow your edits: retype or reshape something and its outline, shadow, glow or bevel updates too
- Picture Editor: a new Transparency tab removes white backgrounds and colors, and makes cut-outs
- New guide buttons put a guide on the top, center, bottom or side of what is selected
- Existing .lyt layouts are never overwritten: Save always makes a .dsm, and Export asks for a new name
- Soft shadows, glows, feathering, see-through colors and gradients now come in from .lyt layouts
- Edit in...: open a picture in another program and it comes back updated when you save there
- Align works on a single object against the page; guides can snap to objects; drag a guide onto its ruler to remove it
